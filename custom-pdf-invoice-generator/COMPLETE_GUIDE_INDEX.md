### "PDF Download Isn't Working"
1. Open: PDF_TROUBLESHOOTING.md

**Total time:** 5 minutes

---

### "I Want to Change the Logo"
1. Open: QUICK_LOGO_CHANGE.md (fast way)
2. Or: HOW_TO_CHANGE_LOGO.md (detailed)
3. Add logo to `public/` folder
4. Update 2 files
5. Done!

**Total time:** 5-15 minutes

1. Open: HOW_TO_ADD_COMPANY.md
2. Click "+ Add New Customer"
3. Select "Company" button
4. Enter details
5. Keep checkbox checked
6. Generate invoice

**Total time:** 2 minutes
