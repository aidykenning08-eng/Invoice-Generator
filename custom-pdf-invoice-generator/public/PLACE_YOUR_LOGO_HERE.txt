📍 PLACE YOUR LOGO HERE

Your logo file should be named: logo.webp

Instructions:
1. Save your logo as "logo.webp" (WebP format)
2. Delete this text file
3. Put your logo.webp file in this folder (public/)
4. That's it! The app will automatically use it.

Logo Requirements:
- Name: logo.webp (exactly this name)
- Format: WebP (recommended) or PNG
- Size: 400-600px wide recommended
- Transparent background preferred
- File size: Under 200KB recommended

How to convert your logo to WebP:
- Online: https://cloudconvert.com/png-to-webp
- Or just rename your PNG to logo.webp (works too)

After adding your logo:
- Test locally: npm run dev
- Upload to GitHub
- Vercel will auto-deploy with your logo!
