# How to Add a Company to the Dropdown

Follow these simple steps to save a company so it appears in the dropdown list:

## Step-by-Step Guide:

### 1. Select "Add New Customer"
- At the top, click the dropdown menu
- Select **"+ Add New Customer"**

### 2. Choose "Company" Type
- You'll see two big buttons: **"Individual Customer"** and **"Company"**
- Click the **"Company"** button (it will turn blue)

### 3. Enter Company Information
- Fill in the **Company Name** (required) - e.g., "ABC Motors (Pty) Ltd"
- Add Email, Phone, and Address (optional but recommended)

### 4. Make Sure "Save" is Checked
- You'll see a checkbox that says: **"Save this customer to the dropdown list"**
- This checkbox is **checked by default** ✅
- Leave it checked!

### 5. Generate Your Invoice
- Fill in the invoice items/services
- Click **"Generate Invoice"**
- The company will be **automatically saved** to the dropdown!

---

## Alternative: Save Without Generating Invoice

If you just want to add the company to the list without creating an invoice yet:

1. Follow steps 1-3 above
2. **Uncheck** the "Save this customer" checkbox
3. Click the green **"💾 Save Customer to Dropdown"** button
4. Done! The company is now saved and will appear in the dropdown

---

## Next Time You Need to Invoice That Company:

1. Click the dropdown at the top
2. Select the company name from the list
3. All their details will fill in automatically!
4. Just add your invoice items and generate!

---

## Example:

**Adding "Toyota Dealership":**
1. Select: "+ Add New Customer"
2. Click: "Company" button
3. Enter: "Toyota Dealership" in Company Name
4. Enter: Email, phone, address
5. Keep checkbox ✅ checked
6. Click: "Generate Invoice"

**Next invoice for Toyota:**
1. Select: "Toyota Dealership" from dropdown
2. Details fill automatically
3. Add items and generate!

Easy! 🚗✨
